                           Add a targeted device to a group
     -r REMOVE_GROUP, --remove REMOVE_GROUP
                           Remove a targeted device from a group
     -l LIST_GROUP, --list LIST_GROUP
                           List device in target group
     -dev DEVICE_ID [DEVICE_ID ...], --device-id DEVICE_ID [DEVICE_ID ...], --device_id DEVICE_ID [DEVICE_ID ...]
