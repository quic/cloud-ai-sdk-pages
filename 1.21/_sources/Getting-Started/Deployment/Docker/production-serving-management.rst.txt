Production Serving and Management
=================================

These images support **production deployments** and infrastructure integration.

- ``cloud_ai_k8s_device_plugin``  
  Exposes AIC100 cards to Kubernetes schedulers.

- ``cloud_ai_triton_server``  
  Enables Triton Inference Server-based deployments.

- ``cloud_ai_mgmt_aicm`` and ``cloud_ai_mgmt_qmonitor``  
  Provide device management and monitoring capabilities.
